# Scoped prior-art and scientific limitations

Research date: 2026-10-07. This experiment explores candidate lithium-ion battery cathode stoichiometries in `Li2Mn_(1-t-n)Ti_tNb_nO2F`. It is a different application and chemistry from the earlier aluminum heat-spreader study. The scientific family, the two principal endpoint controls, and the idea of combining manganese, titanium and niobium in a disordered-rocksalt oxyfluoride are already established.

The search used Crossref bibliographic/title queries and OpenAlex searches, followed by retrieval of publisher abstracts and accessible author manuscripts. Queries included “Reversible Mn2+ Mn4+ double redox lithium excess,” “manganese titanium niobium oxyfluoride cathode,” “Li Mn Ti Nb oxyfluoride cathodes,” “high entropy cation disordered rocksalt cathodes,” and “manganese oxyfluoride short range order fluorination.” Fractional formula strings were also searched. These services do not reliably index exact stoichiometries, so an absent hit does not establish novelty. This is a scoped literature review, not an exhaustive full-text or patent search. ## What the screen can and cannot establish

Formal charge balance determines an average Mn valence only after the other species' charges are assumed. It does not determine local oxidation states, charge transfer, coordination, crystal structure, anion vacancies or phase segregation. The electron count to formal Mn4+ gives a theoretical Mn-redox reservoir per mass of pristine nominal active material. It does not predict a voltage curve, delivered capacity, energy density, rate capability, cycle life, electrode or cell performance. In particular, a larger reservoir can be inaccessible because of structure and kinetics, while oxygen redox can produce capacity beyond the Mn-only count.

No generated formula or integer supercell representation demonstrates a stable or synthesizable disordered-rocksalt phase. Lattice dimensions, density, short-range order and atomic configurations require separate evidence. An ideal random arrangement, if later supplied for modeling, would still be a hypothesis rather than a measured structure.

The defensible output is a reproducible shortlist of stoichiometric hypotheses and controls within a known research family. Neither exact-recipe novelty nor improved real battery performance is established by this search or screen.

## Evidence needed next

Before recommending a new cathode, compare candidate and endpoint controls using matched synthesis and electrode conditions. Establish actual elemental and fluorine content, phase fractions, local structure and oxidation states. Computational follow-up should examine competing phases, disorder ensembles and lithium migration rather than optimize only electron count. Experimental follow-up should measure voltage-dependent reversible capacity, coulombic efficiency, rate response and repeated-cycle retention with declared active-material loading and voltage/current conditions. Track oxygen activity, gas evolution, electrolyte decomposition and metal dissolution. Exact-composition full-text and patent searches remain necessary before making a novelty claim.
